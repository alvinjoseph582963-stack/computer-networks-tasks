What is a Computer Network?

A simple definition to get started.

A computer network is a group of computers and other devices connected together so they can communicate and share resources.

Think about it: When you send a WhatsApp message, browse a website, or connect your laptop to Wi-Fi, devices are communicating across a network.

Types of Networks

Networks can be classified by the area they cover.

LAN

Local Area Network

Covers a small area, such as one building.

Example: Computers connected in a school computer lab.
MAN

Metropolitan Area Network

Covers a city or a large campus.

Example: A network connecting different offices across a city.
WAN

Wide Area Network

Covers a very large geographical area.

Example: The Internet.
